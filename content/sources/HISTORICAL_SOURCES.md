## Attribution and limits

- Catalog source records credit the publishing institutions and identified authors. Original prose is summarized; no document, image, model or other media is redistributed by this task. A citation is not an asset license.
- Creator lists identify relevant contributions, not every participant. They must not become claims of exclusive individual invention.
- Avoid absolute “first computer” claims, numerical claims about shortening the war, and promotional claims of guaranteed commercial outcomes.
- Educational descriptions deliberately omit circuitry and operational detail. Future demos need independently reviewed scripts; the simplified descriptions do not approve any simulation behavior.
- Future Enigma variant, Bombe configuration, Colossus reconstruction, ENIAC phase and model-specific visual details still require asset-level verification. Existing fidelity labels do not certify a reconstruction.
- Credits remain empty because no exhibit asset rights were established. Hotspots, model assets, markers and demonstrations remain unfinished. Release validation is expected to fail on missing credits.
